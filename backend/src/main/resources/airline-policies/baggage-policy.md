# SkyBook Sample Policy: Baggage Allowance

This is a sample policy used for AeroMate demonstrations.

- Economy passengers can carry one cabin bag up to 7 kg.
- Business passengers can carry two cabin bags up to 14 kg total.
- Checked baggage allowance depends on fare family and route.
- Oversized or overweight baggage may incur additional charges.
- Restricted items are not permitted in cabin or checked baggage.

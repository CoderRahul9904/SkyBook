# SkyBook Sample Policy: Check-In Guidelines

This is a sample policy used for AeroMate demonstrations.

- Passengers should complete check-in before departure cutoff.
- Web check-in opens 24 hours before departure for most flights.
- Airport check-in counters may close 45 to 60 minutes before departure.
- Government-issued identification is required.
- Boarding gate closes before departure and late passengers may be denied boarding.
